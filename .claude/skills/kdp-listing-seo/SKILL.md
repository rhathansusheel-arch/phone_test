# KDP Listing SEO (KDP Agent rules)

Goal: a listing that appears in the Amazon searches real buyers type, and convinces them to buy.
Amazon ranks on (1) words in the title, subtitle and 7 keyword boxes, (2) categories, (3) sales history.

## Research (done by the agent with the research browser profile)
- Amazon search suggestions: seed phrase, seed + each letter a-z, seed + "for", seed + audience words
  ("for adults", "for seniors", "for toddlers", "for kids ages").
- Top 10 books for the main phrase: title pattern, price, pages, reviews, BSR.
  Lower BSR = more sales. If page 1 is all books with thousands of reviews, target a narrower phrase.
- Never copy a competitor's title, art or description, and never use their names.
- Keyword bank grouped by intent: primary phrase, secondary phrases, audience words, occasion words,
  theme/style words, spelling variants (coloring / colouring).

## Title and subtitle
- Title contains the main search phrase (or its core words) naturally, and matches the cover EXACTLY
  (the agent prints the cover and title page from this same title).
- Subtitle = a readable phrase with the next best phrases, not a word list. It also goes on the cover.
- Title + subtitle under 200 characters.
- Not allowed: "best", "bestselling", "#1", "free", "new", years, other authors or book titles,
  trademarks or characters (Disney, Bluey, Pokemon, Crayola, etc.), repeated generic words, HTML,
  misleading claims.

## 7 keyword boxes
- Each box = a different phrase buyers really type, in natural word order, up to 50 characters.
- No words already in the title, subtitle, author name or chosen categories (Amazon already indexes those).
- No commas, quotation marks, brands, author names, "Kindle Unlimited", "best", "new" or years.
- Cover synonyms, audiences, occasions, themes, spelling variants and long-tail phrases from the research.

## 3 categories
- The 3 most specific categories that truly fit, written as KDP's browse path
  (e.g. "Juvenile Nonfiction > Activity Books > Coloring").
- Prefer categories whose top books are beatable (fewer reviews) over crowded ones.

## Description
- Line 1: a short hook about the benefit or feeling.
- 1-2 sentences on what the book is and who it is for, using the main phrases naturally.
- "Inside you'll find:" followed by 5-7 bullets with numbers (e.g. 40 pictures, 8.5 x 11 in pages,
  one picture per page with blank backs, thick bold lines, colour test page).
- End with a gift line (who to give it to, occasions).
- Use both "coloring" and "colouring" where it fits naturally.
- Plain, honest language. No bestseller claims, no medical claims ("relaxing" is fine, "cures anxiety" is not).
- Every claim must be true of THIS book (use only the book facts given).

## Price and royalty
- Match top competitors. Royalty per sale = rate x list price - printing cost.
  Rate is 60% at $9.99 or more, 50% below (Amazon.com). Printing cost from KDP's printing-cost numbers.
- Give 2 price options with royalty per sale for each, and recommend one.
- Price must be above KDP's minimum (royalty above zero).

## Final check
- Title matches the cover exactly; subtitle is on the cover.
- Title + subtitle under 200 characters, no banned words or trademarks.
- 7 keyword boxes, no title/subtitle/author/category words repeated, no commas or brands, each a different intent.
- 3 specific categories.
- Every claim in the description is true of the book.
- Price and royalty per sale shown.
- AI disclosure on KDP is answered honestly: Yes for images (Claude by Anthropic), Yes for text written by Claude.
